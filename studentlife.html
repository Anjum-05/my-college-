STUDENT LIFE

About student life

Student life at Pragnya Degree & PG College in Chandanagar, Hyderabad balances structured academics with active campus events, sports, and practical exposure. The institution operates both Co-Ed and Women’s campuses and holds an average student rating of 4.1 out of 5 across major review platforms

1.academic flexibility

course variety: • B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce). Focuses on Computer Applications, blending core accounting and finance with practical IT skills.

• BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration). Covers business management, leadership, and entrepreneurial skills.

• BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications). Concentrates on software development, programming, and modern computer applications.

teaching methods: teachers use advance teaching methods and modern technology such as smart classrooms and computer labs

Academic support: Bridge courses and remedial coaching are provided to help students improve their academic performance

2.Skill Development

Pragnya Degree & PG College uses a three-year, phased skill-development roadmap built directly into its regular academic schedule. Instead of waiting until graduation, the college pairs classroom learning with soft skills, internships, and career readiness from day one

3.Festivals and Events

• Pragnyotsav. The college's signature annual cultural and academic festival features stage performances, talent competitions, and award ceremonies honoring student achievements.

• Freshers & Farewell Parties. Students organize lively welcome celebrations for incoming first-year batches and send-off events featuring music, dance, and batch titles for graduating seniors.

• Food Fests & Talent Competitions. Periodic campus food festivals and inter-college competitions give students opportunities to organize stalls, manage events, and compete in arts and debates.

4.internship and Industry Exposure

• Annual Campus Job Fests. The college hosts multi-employer walk-in job fairs in Chandanagar across sectors including IT, banking, HR, BPO, and pharma/healthcare.

• Recruiting Companies. Past campus placements and drives have featured recruiters such as TCS, Amazon, Mahindra, Myntra, Bajaj, and DLF Group, with reported packages typically ranging between ₹1 LPA and ₹5 LPA.

• Workshops & Industrial Visits. As noted on Justdial, the dedicated placement cell organizes regular seminars, skill workshops, and industrial visits to familiarize students with corporate expectations before final-year interviews.
