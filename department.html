Computer Science

computer science is the study of computation,information and automation,focusing on the theory. A computer is an electronic device that processes raw data into meaningful information at remarkable speeds. Originally conceptualized in the 19th century by Charles Babbage and later realized through early machines like the ENIAC, computers have evolved from massive, room-sized calculators into sleek laptops, tablets, and smartphones. Today, they serve as the backbone of modern society, transforming how we work, learn, and communicate.

Mathematics

Mathematics is the universal language of logic, patterns, and numbers that helps us understand and navigate the world around us. From ancient civilizations using basic arithmetic for trade and astronomy to modern scientists modeling complex quantum systems, mathematics has always been at the heart of human progress. Far more than just a school subject, it is a fundamental tool for problem-solving.

B.COM

Commerce is the organized system of buying, selling, and exchanging goods and services between individuals, businesses, and nations. Rooted in ancient barter systems and early trade routes, it has evolved into a complex, interconnected global network. Today, commerce serves as the lifeblood of the modern economy, driving production, generating employment, and connecting producers with consumers across the world.
